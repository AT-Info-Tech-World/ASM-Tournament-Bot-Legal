## 3. What Apex Vanguard™ Is

**Apex Vanguard™** is a Discord application designed to support tournament operations, server administration, moderation, scheduling, match management, staff operations, records, notifications, and related server utilities.

The Bot was developed for the **Apex Syndicate of Malaysia™** tournament ecosystem, but its technical operation is not inherently limited to Malaysia.

Apex Vanguard™ may be installed or used in Discord servers in different countries and regions, subject to:

- Discord's platform rules
- applicable law
- server administrator authorization
- Bot permissions
- feature availability
- configuration of the individual Discord server
- availability of required third-party services

The Bot does not represent that every feature is available in every server.

# 4. Full Operational Scope

The following describes the functional areas Apex Vanguard™ may support.

Feature availability depends on configuration and permissions. A feature being described here does not mean that it is enabled in every server.

## 4.1 Tournament Management

Apex Vanguard™ can support tournament administration through configured tournament records.

Depending on configuration, tournament management can include:

- Creating tournaments
- Editing tournament configuration
- Viewing tournament information
- Listing configured tournaments
- Removing tournament records
- Assigning tournament roles
- Connecting tournament records to external bracket systems
- Connecting tournament records to participant spreadsheets
- Configuring tournament channels
- Configuring ticket categories
- Configuring automatic room creation
- Managing tournament specific operational settings

Tournament configuration can contain information such as tournament name, tournament type, external bracket identifiers, spreadsheet references, Discord roles, Discord channels, and other operational settings.

## 4.2 Tournament Types and Competition Formats

Apex Vanguard™ is designed to support tournament workflows that may include formats such as:

- 1v1
- 2v2
- 3v3
- 4v4
- 5v5
- Single elimination
- Double elimination

The exact rules of an individual competition are controlled by the tournament organizer and the configuration used for that tournament.

The Bot does not independently determine whether a tournament's competitive rules are fair, valid, or appropriate.
