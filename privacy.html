1. Scope and Privacy Framework

Global service and regional privacy laws. Apex Vanguard™ is intended for use by Discord communities and tournament operations worldwide, including users in India, Malaysia, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, the United States, Canada, Australia, Brazil and other jurisdictions. This Policy is written to operate as a global privacy notice. The privacy rights, obligations, lawful bases, consent requirements, retention rules, breach duties, representative requirements and other protections that apply to a particular person or processing activity depend on the applicable law and the circumstances of that processing.

This Privacy Policy explains how Apex Vanguard™ handles information when you use the Bot in a Discord server or when a configured integration is used. It covers what we collect, why we use it, who may receive it, how long we keep it and what you can ask us to do with it.

We try to collect only what the Bot needs for the job it is performing, keep access limited to people and systems that need it and use reasonable security measures to protect the information we hold. Where Malaysian personal data protection law applies to the relevant processing, the Operator intends to handle personal data in accordance with applicable requirements, including the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 and applicable amendments, regulations, standards, guidance and regulatory directions. Which legal requirements apply can depend on who is operating the service, what information is involved and where the people or systems concerned are located.

2. Operator and Data Controller

Apex Vanguard™ is operated within the Apex Syndicate of Malaysia™ ecosystem. The party responsible for a particular piece of processing can depend on how the Bot is being used, who controls the relevant Discord server or tournament and which law applies.

Where the Operator is acting as a data controller under applicable law, it determines the purposes and means of the relevant processing. Where the Bot processes information solely on instructions from a Server administrator or tournament organizer, the legal relationship may differ.

This page is meant to tell you, in practical terms, how the Bot handles information. It does not claim that every registration, appointment or statutory role applies to every server or every use of the Bot.

3. Information We Process

The Bot may process the following categories of information when required by a configured feature:

Discord identifiers such as user IDs, guild IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, message IDs and interaction IDs.

Discord profile information such as usernames, display names, server nicknames and avatars where Discord makes that information available to the Bot.

Server information such as server name, server icon, channel configuration, role configuration, membership related metadata and configured feature settings.

Tournament information such as tournament names, participant identifiers, team information, match records, brackets, schedules, results, attendance, Judge and Recorder assignments, recordings and related operational records.

Submitted information such as ticket content, form responses, modal responses, recording links, score information, correction information and other information intentionally provided through Bot features.

Configuration information such as role IDs, channel IDs, tournament settings, recurring settings, staff settings and integration configuration.

Technical and security information such as timestamps, error information, operation metadata, audit information and limited diagnostic information required to maintain reliability, prevent abuse and investigate incidents.

The Bot is not intended to request passwords, authentication tokens, payment card details, private keys or API secrets from ordinary users. Users should never submit such credentials through the Bot.

4. Sources of Information

Information may be received directly from a user through slash commands, buttons, select menus, modals, tickets or other interactions.

Information may also be received from Discord through authorized API and gateway access required for Bot functionality.

Where configured, information may be received from or transmitted to third party services such as Challonge and Google Sheets. Information may also be generated by the Bot through calculations, schedules, attendance processing, result workflows, reminders, logs and other automated operations.

5. Purposes and Lawful Handling

Information may be processed to operate and maintain Apex Vanguard™, authenticate and identify users, enforce permissions, manage Server configuration, operate tournaments and matches, process attendance, assign staff, manage tickets, process scores and results, send reminders and operational notifications, maintain records, generate requested exports, operate managed webhooks, prevent abuse and unauthorized access, investigate security incidents, maintain service reliability, comply with applicable law and respond to valid legal requests.

Where applicable law requires a specific legal basis, notice, consent or other condition, the Operator will seek to apply the requirement appropriate to the processing. The Bot does not rely on a general statement in this Policy as a substitute for a legally required consent mechanism.

6. Discord Access and Permissions

Apex Vanguard™ operates through Discord and may access information made available through Discord APIs, gateway events, permissions and enabled intents.

The Bot may need access to members, roles, channels, messages, interactions or other Discord resources depending on the feature being used. Access does not mean that every available item is permanently stored.

Discord separately processes information under its own policies. Discord states that third party developers operating bots and applications may process information independently and are expected to maintain their own privacy policies. Users should review Discord's policies as well as this Policy.

7. Storage and Security

Persistent application records may be stored in MongoDB backed infrastructure. The Bot may also use hosting infrastructure, logging systems and other technical services necessary to operate the application.

The Operator applies reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the service, including access control, permission boundaries, credential protection, controlled integrations, error handling, rate limiting, database safeguards and operational monitoring.

No online service can promise perfect security. Please do not put passwords, tokens or other information into the Bot unless the particular workflow genuinely calls for it.

8. Third Party Services

Discord is required for the Bot to operate.

Challonge may receive or provide tournament, participant, bracket, match and result information when a Server or tournament is configured to use Challonge.

Google Sheets may receive or provide participant, attendance, staff or reporting information when a configured workflow uses Google Sheets.

MongoDB and hosting providers may process persistent records and infrastructure data as necessary to provide database and hosting services.

Third party providers operate under their own terms, privacy policies, security practices, retention rules and technical constraints. The Operator does not control independent third party processing.

9. Disclosure and Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be disclosed to Discord and configured integration providers when required to provide a requested feature. Information may also be displayed to authorized Server administrators, tournament organizers, Judges, Recorders or staff when the relevant feature intentionally exposes that information.

Information may be disclosed to infrastructure providers, professional advisers where appropriate, competent authorities where legally required, or other parties where necessary to protect users, Servers, the Bot, infrastructure or legal rights.

We do not use Bot data for unrelated advertising.

10. Retention and Deletion

Retention is determined by the purpose of the information and the operational requirements of the relevant Server or tournament.

Tournament records, schedules, results, attendance, staff records, configuration, audit information and security records may need to remain available for tournament integrity, dispute resolution, security, fraud prevention, operational continuity or legal requirements.

The Operator seeks to avoid retaining personal data longer than necessary for the relevant purpose where applicable law requires such limitation.

Removing Apex Vanguard™ from a Server does not necessarily delete historical records already stored by the Bot or by an external integration. Deletion may require a separate request and may be limited where continued retention is reasonably necessary or legally required.

11. Privacy Rights and Requests

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances of the processing, an individual may have rights concerning access to personal data, correction of inaccurate data, withdrawal of consent where consent is the applicable basis, objection or restriction where legally available, deletion where applicable, and other rights provided by law.

Requests should be made through the official support or administrative channel designated by Apex Syndicate of Malaysia™. The Operator may request reasonable information necessary to verify the request and prevent unauthorized disclosure.

A request may be limited where the requested information cannot be separated from other users' information, where retention is required by law, where deletion would undermine tournament integrity or security, or where another lawful exception applies.

12. Security Incident Handling

If the Operator becomes aware of a security incident involving personal data, it will assess the incident and take steps appropriate to the circumstances and applicable law.

Where Malaysian data protection requirements apply, breach notification and related obligations will be handled in accordance with the applicable legal requirements and regulatory guidance in force at the relevant time.

Users who discover a suspected security vulnerability should report it privately through the official support or administrative channel and should not access unrelated data, alter records or disclose credentials.

13. Children and Age Requirements

Apex Vanguard™ is not designed to independently solicit sensitive personal information from children.

Discord imposes its own age requirements. Users must comply with Discord's applicable age requirements and the laws applicable to them.

If a parent or guardian believes that information was improperly submitted through the Bot, they should contact the Operator through the official support or administrative channel.

14. International Processing

Discord, hosting infrastructure, databases and integration providers may process information in countries other than the country where a user or Server administrator is located.

Where cross border transfer requirements apply, the Operator intends to use the safeguards required by applicable law and relevant regulatory guidance. The precise location and transfer mechanism may depend on the infrastructure and integration provider selected for a particular deployment.

15. Automated Processing

Apex Vanguard™ performs automated operational processing such as permission checks, attendance calculations, schedule processing, reminders, duplicate result checks, webhook management and configured tournament workflows.

These operations are intended to support tournament administration. They are not intended to make legal, employment, credit, insurance or similarly high impact decisions about individuals.

Tournament organizers and authorized staff remain responsible for final decisions concerning eligibility, disputes, competitive outcomes, disciplinary action and other human administrative matters.

16. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Policy may change as Apex Vanguard™ changes. For example, we may update it when we add a feature, introduce an integration, change how information is handled, improve our security practices or need to reflect a change in the law.

When we make a change, the updated version will be posted on this page and the effective date will be changed. If a law requires us to give additional notice or obtain consent for a particular change, we will do that.

Regional privacy laws

Apex Vanguard™ does not treat worldwide availability as a declaration that one privacy statute governs every user. Where a local, national, state, provincial or regional privacy law applies, the Operator will apply the requirements that legally attach to the relevant processing activity.

India. Where applicable, processing of digital personal data involving individuals in India will be handled with regard to the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules 2025 and their applicable commencement, scope and requirements. India has adopted a phased commencement schedule, so individual provisions may become operative at different times.

European Economic Area. Where the EU GDPR applies, the Operator will address applicable GDPR requirements concerning transparency, lawful processing, individual rights, security, retention and international transfers. The GDPR can apply to organisations outside the European Union when its territorial conditions are met.

United Kingdom. Where the UK GDPR and related UK data protection law applies, the Operator will address the requirements applicable to the relevant processing activity, including applicable rights and international transfer requirements.

Malaysia. Where Malaysian personal data protection law applies, the Operator will address the applicable requirements of the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 and applicable amendments, regulations, standards, guidance and regulatory directions.

Other jurisdictions. Depending on the user and processing activity, additional privacy frameworks may apply, including laws in the United States, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Brazil, Japan, Singapore and other jurisdictions. Mandatory rights granted by applicable law are not intended to be excluded by this Policy.

Nothing in this Policy is intended to contractually waive a mandatory privacy right or protection that cannot lawfully be waived. Where a local law provides a specific procedure, deadline, representative requirement or supervisory authority process, that requirement may govern the relevant request or processing activity.
