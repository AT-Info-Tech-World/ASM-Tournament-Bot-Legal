Your privacy matters. This policy explains the types of information ASM Tournament Bot may process when it is used in a Discord server.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to ASM Tournament Bot (“the bot”), operated by Apex Syndicate of Malaysia (“ASM”, “we”, “us”, or “our”). It describes the bot’s handling of information processed through Discord and supported tournament-management integrations.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on features used, the bot may process:

  • Discord identifiers: user, guild/server, channel, role, message, interaction, and related identifiers required for bot functions.
  • Discord profile information: usernames, display names, avatars, and information made available by Discord where needed.
  • Tournament information: tournament names, participant/team information, match identifiers, bracket information, schedules, results, and related records.
  • Attendance and staff information: attendance records, staff assignments, work/match counts, and related operational records.
  • Submitted content: information entered into commands, forms, modals, tickets, or tournament workflows, including recording links where applicable.
  • Configuration information: server-specific settings required for operation.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to provide bot interactions; manage tournaments, schedules, tickets, attendance, and results; manage staff workflows; send reminders and announcements; maintain configuration and persistent state; generate operational logs or exports; prevent invalid or unauthorized operations; and maintain, troubleshoot, secure, and improve the bot.

4. Data Storage

Depending on the feature, operational data may be stored in databases or external systems used by the bot. The current architecture may use MongoDB for persistent application data and Google Sheets or Challonge for supported tournament workflows.

Storage duration can vary by information type, tournament requirements, server configuration, operational needs, and applicable legal obligations. We do not claim that every category of data is retained for one fixed period.

5. Third-Party Services

The bot may interact with Discord, Challonge, Google Sheets, and MongoDB where configured. Third-party providers operate independently and may process information under their own policies and terms.

6. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

The bot is not designed to intentionally request sensitive information such as passwords, payment-card numbers, private authentication credentials, or Discord bot tokens from ordinary users. Do not submit secrets or credentials through bot commands, tickets, forms, or tournament fields.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell user information. Information may be made available to third-party services only where necessary for a configured bot feature or operational workflow, or where required by law. Server administrators and tournament organizers may access information intentionally displayed or exported through bot features.

8. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect operational data and credentials. However, no internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security. Users should avoid submitting passwords, API keys, tokens, financial credentials, or other secrets.

9. Children’s Privacy

The bot is not intended to independently collect personal information from children. Discord has its own age requirements and policies, and users should comply with the requirements applicable to their location and Discord account.

10. Your Choices and Requests

If you believe the bot has stored information that should be corrected or removed, contact the tournament/server administrators or the bot operator through official administrative channels. Some records may need to be retained for tournament integrity, security, dispute resolution, legal compliance, or technical reasons.

11. Discord and Server Administrators

Because the bot operates inside Discord servers, server owners and administrators control many aspects of how the bot is configured and what information is submitted. Removing the bot may stop future processing associated with that server, but does not automatically guarantee deletion of historical records stored by external systems.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the bot’s features, integrations, data practices, or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date indicates the current version.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the operator through the official Apex Syndicate of Malaysia administrative channels or the contact method provided with the bot’s official deployment.

14. Acknowledgement

By using ASM Tournament Bot, you acknowledge that you have had an opportunity to review this Privacy Policy.
