1. Scope and Controller

This Privacy Policy applies to ASM Tournament Bot (“Bot”), operated for Apex Syndicate of Malaysia (“ASM”, “we”, “us”, or “our”). It applies to information processed through the Bot's Discord application, its database-backed services, and integrations used to provide configured tournament and server-management functionality.

Discord itself is a separate service. Discord's own privacy practices apply to information Discord collects directly through the Discord platform. This policy explains the Bot operator's processing, not Discord's processing.

The Bot is not designed to request passwords, Discord authentication tokens, payment-card information, private keys, or API secrets from ordinary users. Users must not submit such secrets through Bot interactions.

3. Where Information Comes From

Information may come directly from users through slash commands, buttons, select menus, modals, tickets, or other interactions. It may also come from Discord's API as necessary for authorized Bot functionality, tournament staff configuration, or connected services such as Challonge or Google Sheets when configured.

4. How We Use Information

We process information for specific operational purposes, including:

  • Authenticating and identifying users within Bot interactions.
  • Checking permissions and enforcing staff or owner restrictions.
  • Creating and managing tournaments, matches, schedules, tickets, attendance and results.
  • Assigning Judges and Recorders and maintaining staff workflows.
  • Sending reminders, announcements, recurring communications and operational notifications.
  • Generating tournament exports and operational reports where requested.
  • Maintaining server-specific configuration and recurring settings.
  • Creating, maintaining, and recovering managed Discord webhooks used for operational logging.
  • Preventing duplicate, invalid, abusive, unauthorized, or malicious operations.
  • Diagnosing failures, maintaining reliability, and improving technical operation.
  • Complying with legal obligations or responding to lawful requests where required.

We do not use Bot data to train general-purpose artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.

5. Discord Access and Permissions

The Bot can access information and perform actions made available by Discord's APIs, authorization, gateway intents, Discord permissions, and Server configuration.

Depending on enabled features, the Bot may need access to server members, roles, channels, messages, or other Discord resources. Access does not mean every available piece of information is permanently stored. Information is processed according to the operational need of the relevant feature.

Discord has announced updated requirements for applications accessing certain server data, including additional review requirements for applications reaching specified user thresholds and annual reviews for certain privileged data access. We will comply with applicable Discord developer requirements.

6. Storage and Infrastructure

Persistent application data may be stored in MongoDB-backed infrastructure. Tournament workflows may also use external systems such as Google Sheets and Challonge where configured.

The precise physical location of infrastructure can depend on hosting and service-provider configuration. We do not represent that all processing occurs in one country.

We apply reasonable access controls and operational safeguards appropriate to the service. No database, network, or online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Third-Party Services

Discord

The Bot runs on Discord and uses Discord APIs. Discord independently processes information under its own policies.

Challonge

Where a tournament is connected to Challonge, relevant tournament and match information may be transmitted to or retrieved from Challonge to operate bracket workflows.

Google Sheets

Where configured, participant, attendance, staff, or reporting information may be read from or written to Google Sheets for tournament operations.

MongoDB / Hosting Providers

Persistent records may be processed through MongoDB and infrastructure used to host the Bot and database. Such providers may process data as necessary to provide infrastructure services.

Third-party services have their own terms, privacy policies, security practices, retention rules, and availability. We do not control those independent services.

8. Disclosure and Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be disclosed or made available to Discord and other service providers when required for configured functionality; to tournament organizers or authorized Server staff when features intentionally display or export information; to infrastructure and integration providers; when necessary to protect users, servers, infrastructure, or third-party rights; or when required by applicable law or valid legal process.

We do not authorize third parties to use Bot data for unrelated advertising purposes.

9. Retention and Deletion

Retention depends on the nature and purpose of the information. Tournament records, schedules, results, attendance, staff records, configuration, audit information, and security records may need to remain available after a tournament or after the Bot is removed from a Server for legitimate operational, integrity, dispute-resolution, security, or legal purposes.

We aim to retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for its purpose, subject to legitimate retention requirements. Some information may be stored in third-party systems and may have separate retention controls.

Removing the Bot from a Server does not automatically erase historical data already stored by the Bot or connected services.

10. Data Requests

For questions or requests concerning information processed by the Bot, contact the Developer Team. Depending on the request, we may need enough information to verify the relevant Server, account, tournament record, or requestor before taking action.

Requests may include asking what categories of Bot data may relate to you, requesting correction of inaccurate operational information, requesting deletion where appropriate and legally available, asking about retention, or raising a privacy/security concern.

Some deletion requests may be limited where records are required for tournament integrity, fraud prevention, security, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or another legitimate operational purpose.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect Bot data and credentials against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Security measures may include access controls, permission boundaries, credential protection, error handling, controlled integrations, and operational monitoring.

Security is not absolute. If you discover a vulnerability, do not attempt to access unrelated data, delete evidence, or disclose credentials publicly. Contact the Developer Team with the relevant details so the issue can be investigated responsibly.

12. Children and Age Requirements

The Bot is not designed to independently solicit sensitive personal information from children. Users must comply with Discord's applicable age requirements and laws applicable to them. If a parent or guardian believes information has been improperly submitted through the Bot, they should contact the Developer Team.

13. International Processing

Because Discord, hosting infrastructure, databases, and third-party integrations may operate across different countries, information may be processed outside the country where a user or Server administrator is located. Where applicable, processing and transfers are subject to safeguards required by law and the terms of relevant service providers.

14. Automated Processing and Decisions

The Bot performs automated operational actions such as permission checks, attendance calculations, schedule processing, reminders, duplicate-result checks, webhook management, and configured tournament workflows.

These automated operations support tournament administration and are not intended to make legal, employment, credit, insurance, or similarly high-impact decisions about individuals. Tournament staff may make final human decisions concerning competitive outcomes, eligibility, disputes, or disciplinary actions.

15. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Bot's features, integrations, data practices, security practices, or legal requirements change. The current version remains available at this URL and displays an updated effective/last-updated date.

16. Contact

For privacy questions, data requests, security reports, or concerns about the Bot's handling of information, please contact the Developer Team through the official support or administrative channel provided by the Bot operator.

This Privacy Policy is intended to describe the Bot's operational data practices based on its current design. It is not legal advice. If the Bot is operated commercially or in jurisdictions with specific privacy obligations, qualified legal counsel should review this policy and the underlying data-processing arrangements.
